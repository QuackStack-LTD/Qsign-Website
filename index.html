Capabilities

Qsign gives you full control over how your existing certificate signs a document — from signature placement to its visual appearance.

Custom Placement

Drag and drop the signature field to any position on any page. Place it exactly where it needs to be — with pixel-level precision.

Adjustable Size

Resize your signature field freely. Whether you need a compact stamp or a full-width block — Qsign adapts to your document's layout.

Background Image

Add a custom background image to your signature — your company logo, a seal, or any branding element that represents you.

Custom Text Overlay

Add text to your signature with template placeholders — signer name, timestamp, and custom messages. Choose from predefined templates or write your own.

PDF Signing

Open any PDF, apply your token's certificate to sign it, and export a signed copy. Built-in PDF viewer with zoom controls for seamless document review.

Signature Verification

Verify any signed document instantly. Check signature validity, signer identity, and the certificate chain — all within the app.

Legal Framework

Built on the
eIDAS standard

Qsign works with certificates that conform to EU Regulation No 910/2014 (eIDAS) — the European standard for electronic identification and trust services. Your certificate lives on your physical token; Qsign is the client that applies it to documents.

Under eIDAS, a Qualified Electronic Signature (QES) has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. It is automatically recognized and legally binding across all 27 EU member states, as well as the EEA countries (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein).

Qsign is fully compatible with B-Trust — Bulgaria's leading Qualified Trust Service Provider operated by BORICA AD — and works with any eIDAS-compliant certificate on a physical token or smart card across the EU.
